## Quick start

Requires Home Assistant 2024.11 or newer and a Timer helper.

1. Open **Marketplace** in Home Assistant 2026.11 or newer, or **HACS** on older versions. Search for **Timer Panel Card**, download it, then refresh your browser.
2. In your dashboard, select **Add card → Timer Panel Card**.
3. Choose your Timer helper and, optionally, a companion entity to show its status.

See the [quick-start guide](https://github.com/JustBeanie/timer-panel-card/wiki/Quick-start) for screenshots and setup details, or follow the [manual installation steps](https://github.com/JustBeanie/timer-panel-card/wiki/Troubleshooting#manual-resource-installation).
